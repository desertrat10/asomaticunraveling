## Thesis

This sequence is not a mobility sequence and does not treat posture as its content. It is an **autonomic headroom intervention** in the sense the thesis doc uses the term: it does not address the structural source of dysafferentation, it lowers the ambient sympathetic floor by loading a channel that is intact.

Three claims hold it together.

**1. The load-bearing channel is respiratory and auditory, not proprioceptive.** Every attention object in this sequence is cardiac, respiratory, or sound-decay. Nothing asks for head-position-in-space, closed-eye balance, or real-time postural self-assessment. This is not caution — it is that afferent data from six degenerated cervical levels cannot validate a posture, so a sequence built to be validated that way is built on nothing. Where position information is needed, it is supplied **externally** (wall contact, floor contact, prop height), not internally.

**2. Endurance here is duration at stable autonomic state, not range, depth, or complexity.** The record contains no cervical ROM value, no JPE score, no HRV, no breath measure, and no interoceptive instrument. There is nothing to progress a sequence *against*. What there is: a twelve-group daily body scan, an autonomic track, and a symptom-onset architecture. So the progression variable is time — the same sequence, held longer, at an unchanged autonomic reading.

**3. Single-variable progression is an epistemic requirement, not a conservative preference.** Symptom onset in this record runs 6–48 hr delayed (8/3 brief), with one real-time exception (7/31). W15 §5d is the demonstration case: three variables landed inside 48 hours — cycle onset, meloxicam initiation, workshop load — and neither the improvement nor the drop can be attributed to any of them. A delayed-onset system with a wide confound surface can only be read if one thing changes per cycle. A sequence that adds duration *and* a new posture *and* a new breath ratio in the same week generates data that cannot be used. The low variance is what makes the practice legible.

**Corollary on what this sequence does not do.** It is not designed to close the gate (Synopsis §III). It is not designed to produce calm as a terminal state. Symptom appearance during or after it is data from a working instrument, not a failure of the sequence, and the monitoring window in §7 exists to capture it rather than to prevent it.

## Block 2 — Transition up · 2 min

Floor-to-standing transitions are a logged dizziness class, 2 occurrences (5/2). This sequence contains exactly **two** transitions, and each is staged.

Four stations, ~25 sec each, eyes open throughout, one hand on the wall from station 3:

1. Supine → side-lying, **right side down** (left shoulder stays unweighted — 2003 capsular shrinkage).
2. Side-lying → seated, pushing from the underside forearm, not from the top hand.
3. Seated → kneeling.
4. Kneeling → standing, hand on wall.

If lightheadedness appears at any station, the station is the endpoint: stay there, and the sequence continues from the floor as a Block 5 extension. That is a structural rule, not a felt-sense read — it triggers on the symptom, not on an assessment.

### Phonation flag — read before running 5.2

Bhrāmarī is the strongest vagal item available that makes no proprioceptive demand and no postural demand. It is also **vocalization**, and high-output speaking is the established driver of the largest cascade in the record: W15's two speaking days produced six upper-corridor groups at 5 for two consecutive days, the highest sustained scan anywhere in the set, with continuous dizziness from the end of the second call — while a nine-hour screen day two days later held a flat 3.

Humming is not speaking. There is no cognitive load, no postural output, no conversational demand, and no sustained cervical position. But the corridor whose driver is phonation load has not been tested against low-load phonation, and this is the difference between a safe assumption and an untested one.

**Treat 5.2 as a probe, not a component.** Do not run it on the same day as any speaking load. If run, it is the only new variable that cycle — per the thesis, §3.

## §7 — Monitoring window

Onset architecture in this record is **6–48 hr delayed**, with one real-time exception (7/31 lumbar seize). The 7/27 session peaked at 48 hr (7/29, pain 6, flu-like fatigue and brain fog) and recovered by 72.

- **In-session:** lightheadedness, spatial disorientation, nausea, visual disturbance → the transition rule in Block 2 applies; the sequence continues from the floor.
- **+24 hr and +48 hr:** twelve-group scan and autonomic track, as the standard log already captures. 48 hr is the read that matters.
- **+72 hr:** clear/not-clear.
- **Nothing else changes in the window.** No new pose, no added duration, no new breath item.

**Progression rule:** if three consecutive runs clear 48 hr with no scan movement above baseline and no autonomic entry, add **duration only** — Block 5.1 and 5.3, one minute each. Not depth, not complexity, not a new posture.
