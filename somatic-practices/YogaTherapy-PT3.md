# A Somatic Unraveling — Modular Yoga Therapy Stack

A modular yoga therapy system built by Crystal Street (500 RYT, Naropa University) for a specific cervical degenerative disc presentation — severe C6–C7, torn disc cartilage at C5–C6, multi-level foraminal narrowing, and reversed lordosis. Developed alongside an active physical therapy protocol, the system uses real-time symptom data, autonomic state, and heart-rate thresholds established during supervised PT cardio sessions to determine what the body can safely load on any given day.

## Design Philosophy

Every block is modular. They can be deployed individually on high-pain days or stacked into a full sequence on low-pain days. The system accounts for:

- **Heart-rate thresholds** set weekly during PT-supervised cardio sessions, with sequences calibrated to either tap or stay below the symptom onset HR
- **Autonomic state tracking** — baseline, elevated, escalating, pain-dominant, or easing — determining which blocks are appropriate
- **Tissue-release timing windows** — 72-hour minimum separation between manual therapy and proprioceptive loading, gated against nausea, visual disturbance, and cognitive fog
- **Proprioceptive vs. vagal channel integrity** — breath work, restorative, yin, and interoceptive work run through an intact vagal channel; proprioceptively demanding postures are gated, not excluded

## Block Categories

### Heart Rate Blocks (HR-B1 through HR-B4)

Designed to work with fast or slow HR increases using Hatha poses selected for cervical compatibility. Each sequence deploys Ujjayi breath to engage the parasympathetic nervous system. HR blocks range from threshold-tapping vinyasa flows to supine recovery, forming the cardiovascular spine of the practice.

- **[HR-B1](https://www.tummee.com/yoga-sequence/Qc0IZ?src=cl)** — Dynamic vinyasa threshold-tapping block. Sūrya Namaskāra A and Virabhadrāsana flows raise heart rate toward and briefly across the symptom threshold, with conditional routing: stationary side plank if symptoms escalate, or sustained Chataraṅga/vinyasa flows at threshold if symptoms hold.
- **[HR-B2](https://www.tummee.com/yoga-sequence/TdYp2)** — Sub-threshold standing flow block. Sustained Warrior and balance sequences (Tree, Warrior III, Half Moon, Dancer, Triangle flow) with dṛṣṭi-gated gaze focus, holding HR 10–15 points below threshold.
- **[HR-B3](https://www.tummee.com/yoga-sequence/kGiTt)** — Seated core and hip block. Slow core engagement (Boat, Bicycle), hip openers (Butterfly, Pigeon), and grounded spinal work (Staff, Ardha Matsyendrasana, Gomukhasana) below threshold.
- **[HR-B4](https://www.tummee.com/yoga-sequence/xDnCc)** — Supine recovery and downregulation block. Gravity-supported postures (legs up wall, supine twists, supported bridge, happy baby) bringing heart rate back toward resting.

### Range of Motion Blocks (ROM-B1, ROM-B2)

Designed to precede HR blocks or stand alone on high-pain days. Focus on gentle cervical mobilization, nerve glides, and upper trap/spine activation. All ROM postures are seated or quadrupedal — no standing balance required.

- **[ROM-B1](https://www.tummee.com/yoga-sequence/svqas)** — Seated foundational neck-warmup block. Neck mobilizations, nerve glides, Eagle Arm trap activations, spinal twists, and seated Cat/Cows with HR at or just above resting.
- **[ROM-B2](https://www.tummee.com/yoga-sequence/aSP4e)** — Quadrupedal transition block. Thread the Needle, Bird Pose flow, Cat/Cow, and Kneeling Cat Swan introducing gentle flow. HR 65–75, bridging ROM into HR sequencing.

### Nervous System Blocks (NS-B1, NS-B2)

Designed to close full or modified sequences, or as standalone practices. Vagal-channel work targeting parasympathetic expansion and autonomic headroom over time.

- **[NS-B1](https://www.tummee.com/yoga-sequence/s1La3)** — Savasana, Sukhāsana, Nadi Shodhana.
- **[NS-B2](https://www.tummee.com/yoga-sequence/In48J)** — Savasana, Sukhāsana, Brahmari (bee breath).

## Typical Stacking Patterns

| Day Type | Stack |
|---|---|
| High-pain day | ROM-B1 → (ROM-B2 if tolerable) → NS-B1 or NS-B2 |
| Moderate day | ROM-B1 → ROM-B2 → HR-B3 → HR-B4 → NS-B1 |
| Full practice day | ROM-B1 → ROM-B2 → HR-B1 or HR-B2 → HR-B3 → HR-B4 → NS-B2 |
