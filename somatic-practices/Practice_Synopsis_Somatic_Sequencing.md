# PRACTICE SYNOPSIS — Somatic Sequencing Project

*Project-knowledge file. Written to be read first, before any sequence is drafted.*

**Purpose of the project:** to design yoga sequences and somatic practices that are anchored to a specific cervical spine condition, driven by HealingOS PT log data, and built for nervous system work rather than postural achievement.

**Purpose of this file:** to establish who the practitioner is, what the body will and won't take, and what this project is not permitted to become.

## I. THE PRACTITIONER

Thirty years of vāmācāra practice preceding and running alongside the degree.

She studied with lineage holders in a rigorous setting specifically so that any later adaptation would rest on understanding rather than aesthetic borrowing. This is the standing behind every position in Section IV. It is not an opinion she holds; it is a decision she paid for.

She built community programming and event containers inside a corporate technology organization — $400K+ event cycles inside an extraction ecosystem. The wellness critique in this project is written from the chair of the person who built the room, not the person who sat in it.

**The third relevant fact:** the cervical injury was sustained during that workplace period, before the whistleblowing. The body broke from containment, not from expression. ## II. THE BODY — CURRENT STRUCTURAL PICTURE

This is the hardware layer. It is permanent and it is the design constraint for everything downstream.

**Structural.** Multi-level cervical degeneration across C2–T1. Six levels of disc degeneration, central canal stenosis, a disc extrusion, reversed cervical lordosis. The mechanoreceptors across those levels are structurally degraded and produce erroneous afferent signaling.

**Autonomic.** The vagal trunk runs through the cervical region; the sympathetic chain ganglia sit in the cervical paraspinal corridor. The structural pathology is therefore *upstream* of autonomic function, not adjacent to it. Baseline sympathetic tone is mechanically elevated by dysafferentation. This is not a psychologically driven activation and must never be sequenced as if it were.

**The presenting pattern: cervicogenic dizziness (CGD).** Continuous mismatch between cervical proprioceptive, vestibular, and visual input. Symptom constellation: lightheadedness, nausea, brain fog, blurred vision, spatial disorientation. Sensorimotor, visual, autonomic, and cognitive — a full picture, not isolated proprioceptive dysfunction.

**Compensation architecture: partially dismantled, not replaced.** A 25-year right-side bracing pattern (upper trap, levator scapulae, rhomboids, thoracic erectors) was upweighted by the nervous system as substitute proprioceptive input after the disc-level mechanoreceptors degraded. PT Round 2 released a significant portion of it. The endurance-building phase did not fully advance. **The old scaffolding came down faster than the new stability went up.** This gap is the current operating condition and explains why autonomic symptoms surfaced *more* in Round 2 than Round 1.

**The governing rule that came out of PT: this is a sequencing contraindication, not an exercise contraindication.** Almost nothing is forbidden outright. What is forbidden is stacking — release followed by proprioceptive load, or load followed by load, without a separating window. Cascade events in the logged record come from stacking, not from any single input.

**Care infrastructure now.** PT has ended. Current architecture is self-governed and multi-modality: craniosacral, deep tissue, yoga therapy under a single-case experimental design (SCED). Full medical clearance from neurosurgeon and PT for training within the boundary-holding frame.

## III. THE PRACTICE AS IT ACTUALLY IS

**It is not āsana-led.** The working sequence architecture is prāṇāyāma-and-dhāraṇā-led, with posture serving as seat and container rather than as content. This is not an accommodation. It is what the Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā actually describes — fifteen āsanas, eleven of them seated or on the floor, occupying one preparatory chapter out of four. Any sequence built here that treats postural flow as the substance of the practice is inverting both the text and the body.

**Established cervical modifications** (from the existing post-PT sequence):
- No upward gaze transitions
- Five or more breaths held per posture — long holds over transitions
- Modified sūrya namaskāra with child's pose replacing downward dog as the resting position
- Controlled postural environment; unsupportive seating is a logged trigger class

**Practice states.** State A and State B are the established shorthand in the HealingOS record — B being the post-release / destabilized window. Sequences must be written per-state, not as a single protocol.

**The dual read.** The same degraded Viśuddhi filter that produces the autonomic symptoms also produces heightened perceptual access — synchronicity, paśyantī-register openness, creative output. This has been documented in live logged events, including significant poetry written during an actively destabilized state. **Sequences must not be designed to close the gate.** The design goal is to modulate load and recovery around a gate that is permanently altered, not to restore a pre-injury filter that no longer exists and is not the target.

**HealingOS three-track logging** is the data substrate:
- Track 1 — musculoskeletal
- Track 2 — neurological / autonomic
- Track 3 — energetic-perceptual (the dual-read track)

Existing capture skills: `pt-support-log` (daily end-of-day body data), `cardio-log` (sub-symptom-threshold sessions under the HR threshold protocol). Sequence design in this project reads from these logs. It does not replace them and does not generate a competing tracking layer.

**The analog channel stays outside the machine.** Hand-written work, movement, community, slow time. Sequences produced here are compost material and structural scaffolding. They are metabolized in the body before they are transmitted to anyone.

## IV. POSITIONS ON APPROPRIATION, COMMODIFICATION, AND WELLNESS THEATRE

These are operating constraints for the project, not background colour. A sequence that violates them is a defective output regardless of how well it is engineered.

### On appropriation

The line is not between using the tradition and not using it. It is between **taking without understanding** and **extracting operational technology with respect**. Tantra itself arose as a democratization of Vedic ritual — a breaking of brahmin gatekeeping. Adaptation is native to the lineage. What is not native is stripping the technology of its epistemology and selling the residue.

The condition on adaptation is practitioner depth. The instrument is only as good as the embodied understanding held by the person using it. This is why the academic lineage study was chosen, and it is why this project may name Sanskrit terms precisely, with full diacritics, in their own conceptual context — and may not use them decoratively.

**Rules for this project:**
- Sanskrit terms are used with precision or not at all. No decorative Sanskrit.
- Traditions are not conflated. Trika, Kaula, Śrīvidyā, and Pātañjala use overlapping vocabulary to different ends; sequences that blend them must say so explicitly.
- Classical citations carry chapter and verse. Where numbering is uncertain across editions, that is stated rather than smoothed over.
- No invented lineage authority. Practices adapted here are adaptations, named as such.

### On commodification and wellness theatre

The core structural claim, in her language:

**Wellness theatre is kañcuka maintenance.** Meditation rooms inside extractive organizations, breathwork at conferences built on labor exploitation, yoga classes teaching regulation so the body can return to the environment that is deregulating it. What is sold, structurally, is **re-veiling as a service** — buffer restoration so the contraction can keep functioning inside the container. Māyā sold back to the worker as a benefit.

> **Why the capture is structural, not accidental.** No institution funds veil-removal, because a de-veiled workforce is ungovernable. The institution requires the kañcukas intact. Wellness programs inside extraction are therefore committed to the opposite of what the recognition lineage was for, regardless of any individual teacher's intent.

**The medieval precedent removes the innocence defense.** The lineage has been conscripted before. Court tantrism sold śānti-karman to kings — pacification of the sovereign body and realm — while the recognition stream (Somānanda → Utpaladeva → Abhinavagupta → Kṣemarāja) held that the problem was never agitation but non-recognition. Two products, one tradition, opposite directions of service. Corporate mindfulness is the modern śānti-karman. This is not dilution by accident; it is the oldest fork in the tantric road.

**Svātantrya, not calm, is the tradition's telos.** Calm is a side effect the industry mistook for the product.

**The marketplace-yogi objection, answered.** Yes, the accomplished practitioner functions anywhere — the rock and the marketplace, the battlefield Gītā, the Vijñāna Bhairava's ordinary-life dhāraṇās. But classical equanimity was never in service of the container. The direction of service ran from environment to practitioner: chosen ground, consecrated entry, intensity serving transformation. Wellness theatre inverts the vector — unchosen extraction, unconsecrated container, the practitioner's stability harvested as productivity.

**The charge is epistemic, not ahiṃsā-based.** The body's report is a **pramāṇa**. Anesthetizing a means of valid knowledge is epistemic harm; the physical and psychic damage follow from it. The signal is the intelligence.

**The exit gradient, held without moralizing.** Exit is a privilege gradient. Regulation-in-service-of-exit — triage while building the runway out — is categorically different from regulation-as-permanent-adaptation, and both differ from bounded, chosen, protective staying. The paśu/vīra distinction is developmental, not moral; the tradition never shamed the bound for being bound. Wellness theatre's offense is not that it helps bound people survive. It is that it forecloses the recognition that would let them see they are bound, and calls the foreclosure healing.

**Ceremonial containers are not exempt.** The retreat economy produces the performance of shadow work without the facilitation to hold it, and returns people to the same behaviors with a fresh certificate of healing. Bypass in one of its more dangerous forms, because it feels like the opposite.

### What this means for sequence design here

- **Sequences do not optimize for return-to-container.** No practice in this project is designed to make a harmful environment tolerable.
- **Regulation is never the terminal goal.** Regulation is in service of recognition and capacity. If a sequence's only stated outcome is "calm," it is misdesigned.
- **Somatic signal is never suppressed.** Symptoms are data from a functioning instrument, not noise to be quieted. A sequence that reduces symptom *reporting* without addressing load has damaged the instrument.
- **No optimization language, no productivity framing, no wellness register.** Not "boost," not "hack," not "reset your nervous system."
- **Visarjana as design ethic.** Containers built here are consecrated and formally dismissed. Nothing is built to require its builder.

## V. HOW THE PROJECT SHOULD WORK

**Inputs:** HealingOS PT support logs, cardio logs, three-track entries, SCED protocol data, stated training goals, current state (A/B), and the classical reference shelf.

**Outputs:** state-specific sequences and somatic practices with explicit load, recovery-window, and stacking constraints attached; dhāraṇā selections anchored to a somatic door (breath, axis, void, sound-decay, gaze, sensation, gap-state, proprioceptive destabilization, supportless); rationale that names which text and which tradition it comes from.

**Non-negotiable design checks before any sequence ships:**
1. Does it stack release against proprioceptive load without a window?
2. Does it include upward gaze or sustained cervical extension?
3. Does it assume the autonomic activation is psychological rather than mechanical?
4. Does it try to close the gate rather than modulate around it?
5. Does it treat calm as the product?
6. Is any Sanskrit in it doing decorative work?

Any yes is a redesign, not a caveat.

**What the project does not do:** it does not diagnose, it does not override the clinical layer, it does not push toward resolution or closure, and it does not convert composting into protocol before the material has been metabolized in the body. Sequences are offered as structure. What gets practiced is decided by the practitioner and the log.
