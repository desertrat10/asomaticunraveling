# Just a Lost Wednesday.

**Published on [Signals & Seeds](https://signalsandseeds.xyz/just-a-lost-wednesday/)** — 2026

> *Excerpt. Read the full essay on [Signals & Seeds →](https://signalsandseeds.xyz/just-a-lost-wednesday/)*

---

Yesterday was just a Wednesday. Nothing special, nothing abnormal. Just a hump day, 48 hours after my first PT session in 5 weeks. I woke up feeling mostly my self. Some soreness and aches in the regular places my body stores and deploys the pain signals to remind me that I no longer inhabit the body of a 20 year old.

A few hours of reading and writing at my floor desk, designed for perpetual wiggling and sitting like a yogi, and my typical Wednesday turned into a spiral of symptoms emerging in a cascade that moved me to the couch and reminded me that I, in fact, now inhabit a 51 year old body that is broken from the actions of that 20 year old me.

When you're faced with a chronic situation of the body that you no longer can control or predict, your life goes through a series of perpetual changes when you slowly see what Was surrender to what Is and it triggers an ongoing cycle of anger --> rage --> despair --> grief. Over time, you are always looking up from a mountain of loss, and if you aren't careful, the weight will smother the few days of goodness that slip through that relentless cycle.

And on a typical Wednesday, when I careen from functioning human to a broken avatar of my former self, the mountain of loss is harder to crawl out from under than the mountain of pain.

Each hour surrendered to the fog of opioids because the fog of my brain is too much to bear, each meal skipped because standing at my stove for 30 minutes to cook is too much tax to pay for sustenance, each writing session skipped because tapping on a keyboard in that moment is one tax too many to pay-- each loss carries a tax and on a random Wednesday when payment is due.

Bearing witness is something I've built my entire life's work around. I never in a million years thought I'd be turning the skills of the professional witness against myself to make sense of a broken body in order find a clarity in the chaos of it all.
