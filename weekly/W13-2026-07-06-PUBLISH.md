# Weekly Experiment Log — W13 · July 6–12, 2026

**Week descriptor:** The lowest pain range in the set — a narrow band all week — and two clean findings inside it: skipping the pre-emptive afternoon dose produced a spasm cascade, and the first in-person event in two months located a capacity ceiling that has nothing to do with duration.

---

## 1. TL;DR

Pain never broke above the top of the baseline band this week. That's the flattest, lowest week in the record, and it happened with no PT for twenty-five days.

Monday was a neuro hangover from the prior day's composting-and-laser stack, compounded by very poor air quality from smoke. She slept ten or eleven hours, woke tired but clear-headed, then got completely wiped out mid-morning — lucky, as she puts it, that the morning meetings had rescheduled. Waves of energy and kriyas through the afternoon.

Wednesday she took a pre-emptive pain management dose at lunch specifically to head off autonomic symptoms before errands, and nothing surfaced at all — on a heavy screen day with an acute stress spike from last-minute tech changes.

Thursday was the week's best day, low-moderate pain and flagged baseline. She ran the full pre-load protocol before a ninety-minute workshop facilitation — horizontal rig to bank headroom, yin yoga, pain management dose forty-five minutes ahead, pre-load supplement — and it held. Then she did three minutes of laser PT exercises afterward, and set an explicit tracking flag: monitoring the +24 to +48 hour window given the prior week's laser hangover pattern.

**The window landed.** Friday, +24 hours out, the day was relatively pain-free until a cascade came on strong and suddenly — pains starting poolside from sustained head-turning, then right levator scap stabbing in the evening, then a lower back spasm about thirty minutes after that. Stretching didn't touch it. And she caught something else about that day herself: **it was the first day without an early-afternoon pain management dose, and the cascade happened in the absence of that usual pre-emptive dose.** That's a natural experiment on the protocol she'd been running all week.

Sunday was the first in-person event in two months — a community event in a nearby city, thirty people, outdoors, high heat, about six hours door to door. The pre-load held for roughly the first ninety minutes. And the finding is precise: **the real capacity ceiling isn't event duration — it's dense multi-directional standing conversation at close range.** That phase ran about forty-five minutes before a visual-proprioceptive cliff — visual spots during close-range conversation, losing her train of thought, nervous system activation from a close talker. Blurred vision at home, brief nausea on arrival, complete exhaustion. Her words: like running a marathon.

She tags it as a **new trigger profile, distinct from the bar-and-TV mismatch** — this one is social-spatial, generated by the interaction pattern rather than the venue.

One quieter thing worth noting: a full week of consistent deep sleep since reintroducing the sleep-protocol dose.

### 4b. What worked

- **Pre-emptive dosing, on four days.** Wednesday and Thursday both produced days with no autonomic symptoms at all, and neither was a low-demand day — Wednesday carried heavy screen plus acute stress, Thursday carried a ninety-minute facilitation.
- **The workshop pre-load (Thu)** — held the floor through the facilitation on the week's lowest-pain day.
- **The overnight pain management dose plus K-tape (Sat→Sun)** — early hints of a repeat levator and lower-back spasm were in motion and did not escalate.
- **The evening dose on Sat**, taken specifically to prevent a repeat of the previous night's cascade. No repeat.
- **Foam rolling and percussion hook on Tue** — loosened the massive left levator knot.
- **The sleep-protocol dose** — a full week of consistent deep sleep, and it released the Wednesday bedtime back spasm.

### 4c. What didn't work

- **Stretching for the lower back spasm (Fri)** — attempted and did not resolve it; a pain management dose was taken after it failed.
- **The event pre-load did not prevent autonomic depletion (Sun).** It managed pain through the window and held roughly ninety minutes, but she left tired and lightheaded.

### 4d. What made it worse

- **The neuro hangover from the prior day's composting-and-laser stack (Mon)**, compounded by very poor air quality from smoke. Complete wipeout by mid-morning; couch work produced little.
- **Sustained head-turning poolside (Fri)** — where the cascade started.
- **Laser PT exercises (Thu)** — the +24 hour window landed as a cascade.
- **Vacuuming (Sat)** — pain during, mopping skipped.
- **Dense multi-directional standing conversation (Sun)** — ~45 minutes to the visual-proprioceptive cliff.
- **Acute tech stress (Wed)** — her read, not confirmed, as a contributor to that night's 45-minute back spasm.

### 4e. Reactive : generative ratio

**Reactive:** 8 distinct, across all 7 days.
**Generative:** 3 — two yin sessions and one laser session (adverse).
**Behavioral / structural:** 4.

The notable shift this week isn't the ratio, it's **when** the reactive interventions land. Four of the seven days used dosing *pre-emptively* — ahead of a known demand — rather than in response to symptoms. The two days that produced no autonomic symptoms at all are both pre-emptive days, and the one day that skipped the afternoon dose is the day the cascade happened.

### 4f. Open medication / modality tests

- **Pre-emptive afternoon dosing. Natural experiment on Fri — the strongest test this protocol has had.** The dose was run on Tue, Wed, Thu and Sat and **omitted on Fri**, and the log flags the omission itself: the first day without an early-afternoon pain management dose, and the levator → lower back spasm cascade occurred in the absence of that usual pre-emptive dose. Not a designed trial and confounded by the laser window and poolside rotation, but it is an unplanned withdrawal against four adjacent days of administration.

- **Laser PT exercises. Fifth occurrence (Thu), and the first monitored prospectively.** The contraindication was confirmed at N=3 in W10; occurrence four ran the prior week and produced the following day's spike. This time the log set an explicit tracking flag — monitoring the +24 to +48 hr window given the prior week's laser hangover pattern — and the window was then tracked in both following entries. **The cascade landed at +24 hours and resolved by +48.** The prediction and the outcome are both in the record, which is a methodological improvement over occurrence four even though the exercise itself is contraindicated.

- **Pre-load protocol. N=3, and the components differ between deployments.** The workshop variant held completely. The event variant held roughly ninety minutes, managed pain, and did not prevent autonomic depletion. Not the same stack twice, so the three occurrences do not accumulate cleanly.

- **Dense multi-directional standing conversation. N=1, ~45 minute ceiling.** Mechanism stated: constant head rotation plus close-proximity visual convergence. Distinguished from venue-based triggers.

- **Air quality / smoke. N=1**, flagged as possible, never isolated.

- **Heat exposure.** High heat for ~3 hours on Sunday, tagged as a factor and not separable from the event itself.
