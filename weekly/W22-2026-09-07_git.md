# Weekly Experiment Log — W22 · Tue – Mon

**Week descriptor.** Seven consecutive days at baseline, no flare declared, pain held inside a narrow band across 42 checkpoints with none missing. Four self-directed sessions (three yoga, one cardio), one provider session on the final day with no content recorded, zero training days, zero brachial activation on any of seven body scans. 

The week's structural feature is a floor that lifts in the back half: the daily minimum sits at the week's low Tuesday through Friday, rises for the Saturday–Sunday pair, and returns to the low on Monday — while the ceiling is touched on four days and never exceeded. Two days carry non-somatic drivers (a bereavement entering the record midweek; the heaviest community and driving stack since March on Friday), and the two best-documented sessions of the week sit at the bottom of the measured-intensity range and the top of the self-rated-effort range. Wearable coverage is the week's main data loss: resting HR captured on three of seven days, daily HR range on three, no wearable data at all on Friday.
