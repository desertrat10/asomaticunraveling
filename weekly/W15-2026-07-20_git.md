# Weekly Experiment Log — W15 · July 20–26, 2026

**Week descriptor:** A workshop-week cascade on a five-week PT gap — pain to 7–8, six upper-corridor groups pinned at 5 for two consecutive days, then a complete drop to a flat 3 within twenty-four hours of removing speaking load, and a PT booking that fell through mid-cascade.

## 1. TL;DR

The highest sustained body scan in the record, and it broke cleanly into two halves.

Monday jumped to a 7 with the whole upper corridor lighting up and nausea alongside it. The read that day was direct: a month without PT accumulating. K-tape went on at nine and helped a bit. She spent two hours reading at a coffeeshop in soft chairs — not the usual hard-chair-and-laptop setup — and the med let her enjoy it until the pain came back toward the end of the window.

Tuesday eased slightly on meds, and she started a meloxicam trial. The 45-minute break rhythm held through a floor-desk day. A fifteen-minute gym trip went in on the back of a productive day and was, by her own read in the moment, too much for the pain level.

Wednesday and Thursday were the cascade. Two one-on-one calls Wednesday put sharp pain through the right SCM and scalenes, and the pain **broke through the microdose rather than being held by it** — the only day this week that happened. Dizziness ran continuous from the end of the second call. Her cycle started that morning, which usually drops inflammation, and the morning did improve — but it did not survive the afternoon call load. Thursday was a ninety-minute workshop with no breaks and live demos, followed immediately by about three hours of video production: roughly four and a half hours of continuous output on the highest-scanning week to date, on a night of pain-broken sleep.

Then it dropped. Friday — the first of five days off scheduled during Thursday's peak — every group fell to a flat 3 and the autonomic track went quiet for the first time since the cascade. It held Friday even through nine hours of screen time, which points at the speaking load rather than the screen load as this week's driver. One occurrence.

Saturday held there, with the right-side corridor still tagged a half-step above everything else under nothing more than a morning dog walk.

No hands-on care all week. The weekend PT booking fell through Wednesday when the practitioner's calendar turned out not to be live, his onward referral was declined, and the week ends with outreach sent and nothing on the calendar.
