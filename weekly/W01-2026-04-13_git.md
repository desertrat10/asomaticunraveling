## 1. TL;DR

The first logged week, and only four of the seven days are in it. The three missing days include the Wednesday PT session that drove everything that followed.

Thursday opened at 6–7 with the upper traps sore from the previous day's needling and the lower back locked at a 6. All four autonomic markers were present, mild and continuous, worse on head turns and standing. A one-hour Zoom was hard to sit through.

Nothing generative happened this week. Every intervention was release, recovery, or pain management.
