## 1. TL;DR

The first logged week, and only four of the seven days are in it. The three missing days include the Wednesday PT session that drove everything that followed.

Thursday (+24h) opened elevated to near-peak with the upper traps sore from the previous day's needling and the lower back locked at an elevated level. All four autonomic markers were present, mild and continuous, worse on head turns and standing. A one-hour video call was hard to sit through.

Friday (+48h) is where it turned. Left brachial nerve inflammation surfaced in the morning and held all day as a dull ache radiating to the elbow, without progressing to numbness or grip weakness. It was also a heavy client work day, catching up from the recovery days. The floor desk lasted twenty minutes before she moved to a horizontal couch position for the rest of the day. Percussion, foam rollers, and stretching all produced no reset — the first day in the record where the release toolkit did nothing. Autonomic escalated toward evening.

Saturday (+72h) was the peak. Pain peaked, flare classified L2, suboccipitals and temporalis both at top of band, left brachial at top of band on day two. Positional lightheadedness made her stop and pause multiple times. The day itself was light — reading, some computer work on the couch rig.

Sunday (+96h) came down. Flare downgraded to L1, the body scan dropped broadly, the left brachial eased to mid-band. But a new symptom appeared: radiating pangs in the right hand ring and pinky fingertips, noticed in bed in the morning and again walking the dog. Bilateral now. Flagged for the following Monday's PT.

Nothing generative happened this week. Every intervention was release, recovery, or pain management.
