# Weekly Experiment Log — W02 · April 20–26, 2026

**Week descriptor:** A six-day recalibration arc off a substitute-PT session run without needling — autonomic escalating through day 3, first documented dual-task degradation on the travel day, then a clean recovery to a med-free Sunday that included a 1.5-hour coffeeshop session with no reaction.

---

## 1. TL;DR

The first fully logged week, and the most informative one in this set.

Monday was PT with a substitute practitioner, and needling was declined by request — the direct answer to last week's question about spacing. The session was proprioceptor work, manual fascial release on the right traps and scapular region, an autonomic exercise using a laser headlamp against a wall target, and ulnar nerve work. Autonomic symptoms came on about four hours later and escalated through the evening. Nausea appeared in the post-PT cluster for the first time. A scheduled dose cleared the lightheadedness, the nausea, and restored appetite — one dose, multiple symptoms, and the log marks it as the second data point for that effect. Couch-bound by evening, pain peaked, flare L2.

Tuesday (day 1) the scan dropped hard — temporalis and SCM both from elevated to low band. A long morning meeting at the floor desk ran with "no issues, lots of movement."

Wednesday (day 2) undid it. A 1.5-hour coffeeshop session and extended horizontal-rig typing beyond normal duration, on a short night from working late, on recalibration day 2. Autonomic escalated after the coffeeshop and she had to lie down on getting home. Bilateral ulnar nerve irritation appeared, attributed to the typing. That evening she wrote down an intervention rule: ice on the upper traps and suboccipitals when autonomic symptoms are active — vasoconstriction to stabilize local blood pressure, cold to slow nerve conduction velocity and reduce erroneous afferent signal volume — as a peripheral analog to what the scheduled dose does centrally. Heat for purely muscular, ice when autonomic is in the mix.

Thursday (day 3), autonomic came on at two and a half hours of floor desk and stayed all day; an anti-inflammatory and a scheduled dose reduced it without resolving it. The cycle started that day, nine days early.

Friday (day 4) was the functional-capacity test: drive to a nearby city, an hour of seated recording, and manual gear setup. She couldn't hold a conversational thread while setting up gear — the first documented instance of dual-task motor/cognitive degradation. She detected the closing window, skipped the community event she'd planned to attend, and drove home with margin. The log includes a driving load taxonomy and a contrast pair: same route six days earlier, no autonomic response. The variable isn't driving; it's stacking sustained-position transit with cognitive and motor load.

Saturday (day 5) was the best autonomic day of the week despite unplanned physical work. Sunday (day 6) had no autonomic at all and needed no meds — the first genuinely med-free day. And Sunday included a 1.5-hour coffeeshop session with no reaction, against Wednesday's identical duration that produced a crash.

### 4b. What worked

- **The scheduled dose, repeatedly.** Four days, and on Mon a single dose reduced lightheadedness, resolved nausea, and restored appetite together. The log marks this as the second data point for the multi-symptom effect.
- **Real-time load detection on Fri (day 4).** She caught the closing window, dropped the community event, and got home with margin. The only in-the-moment capacity read in the week that worked as intended.
- **Anti-inflammatory on Thu (day 3)** — reduced autonomic and pain, though not to resolution.
- **Frequent movement at the floor desk on Tue (day 1)** — a long morning meeting ran with "no issues."
- **The rest-day structure on Sat (day 5)**, which produced the week's best autonomic day despite unplanned physical work.

### 4c. What didn't work

- **Manual release tools on Tue (day 1)** — percussion gun, hook tool, foam rolling, position changing all logged as maintenance-level with no meaningful reset. Second week running where the toolkit produced nothing during a recalibration window.
- **Rest/lying down after the coffeeshop on Wed (day 2)** — partial only.
- **Anti-inflammatory + scheduled dose on Thu (day 3)** — reduced but "did not fully resolve" the autonomic.

### 4d. What made it worse

- **Monday's PT session itself.** Autonomic onset ~4 hours post-session, escalating through evening to couch-bound. Nausea entered the cluster for the first time. Note that this session ran *without* needling.
- **1.5-hour coffeeshop session + extended horizontal-rig typing beyond normal duration + sleep deficit, on recalibration day 2 (Wed).** Autonomic escalated post-coffeeshop and persisted through evening; had to lie down on returning home. Bilateral ulnar irritation surfaced.
- **2.5 hours of floor desk on Thu (day 3)** — autonomic onset at that mark, persisting all day.
- **The Fri (day 4) stack** — 25-minute surface-road drive, one hour seated recording, manual gear setup, on cycle day 2. First documented dual-task degradation.
