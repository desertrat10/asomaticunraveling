Friday was the payoff: pain 4, no autonomic at all, upper trap and levator scap down to 2. The week recovered to *below* where it started. That does not match the extended recovery tail this record shows for needling-plus-e-stim sessions. She took the tape off Friday evening at 3.5 days, ahead of what she expected to be a quiet weekend.

It wasn't quiet. Saturday the morning read was neutral, so she ran with it — yoga with several chaturangas, cleaning, groceries, hours at the pool, the dog park — and pain and neurological symptoms surfaced by evening. The log calls this out as the textbook version of the pattern the whole system exists to catch: neutral in the morning was not data about the evening.

Sunday was planned low-key and the coffee ran four hours of animated talking. SCM and scalenes both to 4 — the accessory muscles recruited by sustained animated speech — and the most pronounced fog of the week, with word-finding difficulty and trouble holding thoughts.

Two over-load days back to back, from two different mechanisms, on top of more than a week of ongoing PT-arc exhaustion.
