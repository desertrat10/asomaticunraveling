# Weekly Experiment Log — W06 · May 18–24, 2026

**Week descriptor:** A needling-without-e-stim session with a new X-pattern tape produced the cleanest recalibration curve in the record — recovering below the week's starting baseline by Friday — and then the weekend re-loaded it twice, once by choice off a neutral morning read and once by a coffee that ran four hours.

---

## 1. TL;DR

Monday was Day 1 after the drive event. The autonomic cluster came back mild — brief lightheadedness on standing in the evening, and fog through an afternoon coffee — on a big screen day with a remote community event. The coffee was at home, which the log flags as a low-load environment against the coffeeshop variable.

Tuesday was PT and there's no daily log for it, but the session is recoverable. Three things changed at once: needling **without** e-stim, a new X-pattern K-tape geometry, and weighted posterior-pull reps. Autonomic started during the session. The day had zero work demand and produced no fog at all despite strong lightheadedness — which turned out to be half of a useful contrast pair.

Wednesday was the trough. Pain in the upper band, six tissue groups at elevated, the full autonomic constellation at moderate intensity including spatial disorientation. Left anterior shoulder pain showed up in the morning at the scar site — also the X-tape footprint — and was gone by evening. And fog was present, on a moderate screen day. Against Tuesday's no-work/no-fog, that's the cleanest read yet that fog tracks cognitive load layered on cervical state, not the cervical state alone.

Thursday she'd flagged in advance as the risk day — two lead calls plus delivery prep on Day 2 of the recalibration window. It cleared benign. The scan dropped from Wednesday's cluster of elevated readings to uniform mid, pain from the upper band to mid-range, and the only symptom was lightheadedness after each of the two animated calls.

Friday was the payoff: pain in the lower band, no autonomic at all, upper trap and levator scap down to low. The week recovered to *below* where it started. That does not match the extended recovery tail this record shows for needling-plus-e-stim sessions. She took the tape off Friday evening at 3.5 days, ahead of what she expected to be a quiet weekend.

It wasn't quiet. Saturday the morning read was neutral, so she ran with it — yoga with several chaturangas, cleaning, groceries, hours at the pool, the dog park — and pain and neurological symptoms surfaced by evening. The log calls this out as the textbook version of the pattern the whole system exists to catch: neutral in the morning was not data about the evening.

Sunday was planned low-key and the coffee ran four hours of animated talking. SCM and scalenes both to elevated — the accessory muscles recruited by sustained animated speech — and the most pronounced fog of the week, with word-finding difficulty and trouble holding thoughts.

Two over-load days back to back, from two different mechanisms, on top of more than a week of ongoing PT-arc exhaustion.
