# Weekly Experiment Log — W14 · July 13–19, 2026

**Week descriptor:** Two six-hour events in three days, and the second one cliffed in minutes rather than forty-five — establishing that pre-load state, not trigger type, governs time-to-cliff. Three trigeminal signals surfaced in twenty-four hours, one of them a symptom that had been dismissed for an unknown length of time.

---

## 1. TL;DR

This is the week the system stopped recovering between events, and the week the model for why got sharper.

Monday was the decision gate set the prior day — recover enough overnight and the next day's conference is on. The twenty-four-hour comparison was good: autonomic down from a full cliff to mild traces, body scan improving, energy recovering. The gate returned a go.

Tuesday was a six-hour outing to an indoor conference in a nearby city, about three hundred people. The day was staged deliberately — a library coffeeshop reset first, then low-demand social at a bowling alley watching a match, then the seated panel, then light one-on-one networking, then a thirty-minute screen demo from the back of the room. Then the room converted, unplanned, into a three-hundred-person networking event.

**The cliff hit minutes after the conversion.** Speech acceleration and timing corruption, cognitive fog, and a new signal — **left ear ringing.** Stepping outside resolved it immediately.

And the comparison drawn is the most useful thing in the week. The prior event: clean system, forty-five minutes to cliff in dense standing conversation. Tuesday: pre-loaded system after poor sleep and hours of stacked demand, minutes to cliff on a different trigger entirely. **Pre-load time directly correlates with time-to-cliff. The trigger type may matter less than the headroom left when it activates.**

Wednesday the cost arrived in trigeminal territory. Right temporal throbbing on waking with palpable knots around and above the ear, left eye twitching, and the ear ringing back after two hours at the floor desk. Three signals in twenty-four hours, all in trigeminal distribution. **And the ear ringing was reclassified** — previously written off as something trivial like ear wax, now recognised as a cervical signal on the trigeminocervical pathway, and the log notes it has been presenting longer than initially realised, with unknown onset, because it was being dismissed.

Wednesday also produced a clean intervention finding: **K-tape along the right SCM stopped the right temporal throbbing almost immediately.** Mechanism as read — SCM tension pulling on temporal attachments, and the tape offloading enough to break the tension pathway. Applied bilaterally when the left side activated. Logged as claims-grade.

Thursday a kriya/āveśa event came unprompted about two hours into a work block, with a warm buckwheat wrap on the cervical corridor. Body forced horizontal, awareness fully lit, several minutes, clean discharge. Second occurrence in about a month, and both were preceded by the same three conditions: a cervical-corridor intervention, horizontal availability, and a depleted guard. The structural read: it isn't pain level, it's compromise sufficient to open a seam. It doesn't happen on good days.

By Friday, three consecutive recovery days had produced no meaningful improvement in the upper corridor. Saturday the laser exercises were attempted again after foam rolling and hook work — the exact sequence confirmed contraindicated — and near-collapse came inside a minute, then thirty minutes forced horizontal. The match to the contraindication was named in the same entry.

Saturday's midday note closes with two words that matter: **needs massage.**
