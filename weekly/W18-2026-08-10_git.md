# Weekly Experiment Log — W18 · August 10–16, 2026

**Week descriptor:** The first two protocol sessions under the PT's HR-gated plan produced the record's first threshold readings on consecutive days — inside a near-zero-computer-work week that ended with the first community engagement since April, a milder drive than the same route four weeks prior, and a stack tax paid on schedule over a cleared weekend.

---

## 1. TL;DR

**The week the protocol started producing threshold data.**

**Mon** — heavy PT session. Pain peaked for the week, full autonomic presence. Week's high point.
**Tue** — true recovery day. Eased one step.

**Wed — cardio, first threshold reading.** Nausea + dizziness at threshold. **Stopped at 12:50 — symptom-limited.** Pain second wave ~3 hr later on the dog walk.

**Thu — yoga, second threshold** (consistent across two blocks). **Completed in full — symptom-managed**, regulated through with poses and breath. First session with entry *and* exit numbers on the neuro track: **met the +2 criterion.**

> Autonomic was the governing signal both days. Cardio stopped on it; yoga worked through it. Pain trailed both by hours.

**Fri — community event.** First community engagement since May.
- Drive reaction **markedly milder than the same route 4 weeks prior** — first longitudinal driving comparison in the record
- No autonomic at the event; spacey onset waited for the drive home
- ~2 hr 15 min seated total (unplanned café hour + unexpectedly seated presentations) — sequencing miss, not a capacity read

**Sat** — stack tax: right mid trap → temporalis, spikes, one dose of pain management. Mood elevated anyway.
**Sun** — quiet. Cycle day 3, week-low resting HR.

**Week totals:** pain held a moderate-to-high range, no flare escalation. Upper cervical cluster flat across load and rest days alike. Her read: all three experiment days were worth it.

### 4b. What worked

- **The protocol itself** — two sessions, two threshold readings, the record's first.
- **The gating-aware event design** on Fri: early arrival, settle time, dose of pain management at the right moment, exit before the happy hour proper.
- **The cleared weekend behind the stack** — Saturday's tax landed into open recovery space with nothing downstream.
- Standard-dose cadence held the floor all week; no breakthrough-past-med day (contrast W15).

### 4c. What didn't work

- **Live symptom rating during exercise** — the Wed session produced onset HR but no exit numbers; the protocol's delta requirement couldn't be met as written. (Resolved in design: onset-from-zero is self-defining; voice-marking protocol drafted for next sessions.)
- **The unplanned café hour** — an hour of seated reading spent against an event that turned out to be seated. Sequencing miss.

### 4d. What made it worse

- **The Wed/Thu consecutive-day stack** — threshold read lower on day two. Attribution open (see §5d), but the direction is consistent with insufficient spacing.
- **Vacuuming (Sat)** — asymmetric right-side load on an already-activated right side.
- **Computer work, wherever it appeared** — named unprompted on two days as a primary right-side activator.

### 4e. Reactive : generative ratio

**Reactive:** 9 distinct. **Generative:** 2 — both producing protocol data. **Behavioral:** 2.

Against W15 (11 reactive / 1 failed generative / 0 sessions), this is the most generative week in the recent record: two protocol sessions on top of a Monday PT session — one symptom-limited (cardio, stopped on protocol at 12:50), one **symptom-managed and completed in full** (yoga, regulated through with poses and breathing).
