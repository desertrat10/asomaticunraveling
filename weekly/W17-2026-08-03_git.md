# Weekly Experiment Log — W17

**Week descriptor:** Session 2 intensified pain same-day rather than at 48 hours, and the system never returned to the pre-session floor across the following six days — with the right SCM dominant every one of them and the first non-zero brachial readings of the tracking window arriving in the back half.

---

## 1. TL;DR

Session day went bigger than the first one — cupping down the whole spine instead of stopping at mid-thoracic — and the response came on the same day instead of waiting. Pain rose from the low end of the range to the upper end by evening, with a new kind of stabbing around the right scapula, and the body scan hit the highest total of the whole tracking window. That floor never came back. Every day for the rest of the week sat at mid-range or above, which is different from the prior week where the system cleared to low-range by the final day.

The right SCM ran the week. It was the dominant location six days straight, it never dropped below mid-band, and by +96h it was referring up into the temple. The +72h day was the worst: an unplanned solo engagement landed first thing, pain spiked *before* any actual load — just from the notification — and the full neurological constellation held off for the entire ninety minutes and then arrived the second it ended. The cardio session planned for that day got abandoned.

Two things showed up that hadn't been there. The brachial plexus went non-zero for the first time in the tracking window, three separate days, sensory only — no motor change, no grip change. And +120h's TENS session produced the biggest autonomic event of the window: nausea, dizziness, spatial disorientation, involuntary movement, and immediate sleep.

The week's one bright spot is the K-tape. Applied on +96h, it produced the cleanest day of the back half — no real right SCM pain at all except one breakthrough on a stair climb. When it came off the next day, the SCM reactivated.

Three days of rest at the end didn't clear the flare. AQI was elevated all seven days, so there is no clean environmental day anywhere in this window to compare against.
