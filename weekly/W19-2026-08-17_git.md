# Weekly Experiment Log — W19 · August 17–23, 2026

**Week descriptor:** Three loaded days in five with no true recovery day between them — a PT session that pushed five upper groups to the top of band and released to the week's lowest scan 24 hours later, a cardio session run at an already-elevated baseline that breached its own stop criterion and produced a lower-back spasm six hours after, and a yoga session completed clean under the HR ceiling that surfaced a sharp rotation spike afterward: right-only the next day, bilateral by the day after.

## 1. TL;DR

**The week the recovery windows overlapped.**

**Mon — PT session 4.** Manual therapy and cupping on the right-side triggers, some left. Pain went in elevated and came out at the **highest reading in the 30-day window.** Five scan groups at the top of band. *"Nothing is really grabbing this pain."*

**Tue — the one good day.** Twenty-four hours post-PT, the whole scan dropped broadly and pain fell to the week's floor. New couch rig set up that morning; the floor desk then failed at 30 minutes and she shifted to the rig. Large laptop desk arrived that evening. Screen work came back *"without much pain at all."*

**Wed — cardio at an elevated baseline.** Started elevated, 48 hr post-PT, after a long dog walk. 15:47, the longest session in the block. **Autonomic rose from clear to high with onset at minute 9 — the +2 stop criterion — and the session ran to the end anyway.** Coffee and hours of brainstorming after. **Lower back seized that evening** and held overnight. Pain peaked.

**Thu — rebound. Lost day.** Spasm carried into the morning; yin pigeon released some of it. Brain fog present. Pain moderate.

**Fri — PT yoga, clean session.** 29:49 completed entirely in Zone 1, pain flat start to finish, autonomic rose mildly and stayed sub-stop. Thursday's rebound had not cleared before this landed. Then a drive and two hours at a coffee shop, and **two sharp spikes on right rotation — something catching or rubbing at the cervical discs.** New descriptor.

**Sat — rebound, and the spike went bilateral.** Palpable trigger now on the left SCM/scalene with the same rubbing quality. Pain high. Couch day. **Highest HR value in the record — with nothing to attribute it to.** Resting HR up from the week's low on Tuesday.

**Sun — rest.** Spikes diminished, fewer and less intense. Essay published. *"Completely exhausted, depleted."*

> The inversion from W18: last week the two protocol sessions produced the week's **lowest** pain. This week the three loaded days produced the **ceiling** and the two lowest days were the ones with no session in them.

**Week totals:** pain ranged from the week's floor to the highest reading in the 30-day window, mean the highest of the four-week PT round. 2 workouts + 1 PT session consumed 5 of 7 days. One good day, zero true recovery days between sessions.
