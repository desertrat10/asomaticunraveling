# Weekly Experiment Log — W19 · August 17–23, 2026

**Week descriptor:** Three loaded days in five with no true recovery day between them — a PT session that pushed five upper groups to 5 and released to the week's lowest scan 24 hours later, a cardio session run at an already-elevated baseline that breached its own stop criterion and produced a lower-back spasm six hours after, and a yoga session completed clean under the HR ceiling that surfaced a sharp rotation spike afterward: right-only Friday, bilateral by Saturday.

## 1. TL;DR

**The week the recovery windows overlapped.**

**Mon 8/17 — PT session 4.** Manual therapy and cupping on the right-side triggers, some left. Pain went in elevated and came out **7/8+ — the highest reading in the 30-day window.** Five scan groups at 5. *"Nothing is really grabbing this pain."*

**Tue 8/18 — the one good day.** Twenty-four hours post-PT, the whole scan dropped to 2–3 and pain fell to 4/5, the week's floor. New couch rig set up that morning; the floor desk then failed at 30 minutes and she shifted to the rig. Large laptop desk arrived that evening. Screen work came back *"without much pain at all."*

**Wed 8/19 — cardio at an elevated baseline.** Started at pain 6, 48 hr post-PT, after a long dog walk. 15:47, the longest session in the block. **Autonomic 0 → 3–4 with onset at minute 9 — the +2 stop criterion — and the session ran to the end anyway.** Coffee and hours of brainstorming after. **Lower back seized at 6pm** and held overnight. Pain 7.

**Thu 8/20 — rebound. Lost day.** Spasm carried into the morning; yin pigeon released some of it. Brain fog present. Pain 6.

**Fri 8/21 — PT yoga, clean session.** 29:49 completed entirely in Zone 1, pain flat 5 → 5, autonomic 1 → 2 and sub-stop. Thursday's rebound had not cleared before this landed. Then the drive across town and two hours at a coffee shop, and **two sharp spikes on right rotation — something catching or rubbing at the cervical discs.** New descriptor.

**Sat 8/22 — rebound, and the spike went bilateral.** Palpable trigger now on the left SCM/scalene with the same rubbing quality. Pain 7. Couch day. **HR peaked at 151 — highest value in the record — with nothing to attribute it to.** Resting HR up to 65 from 59 on Tuesday.

**Sun 8/23 — rest.** Spikes diminished, fewer and less intense. Essay published. *"Completely exhausted, depleted."*

> The inversion from W18: last week the two protocol sessions produced the week's **lowest** pain. This week the three loaded days produced the **ceiling** (mean 7.0) and the two lowest days were the ones with no session in them.

**Week totals:** pain 4/5–7/8+, mean 6.3 — the highest of the four-week PT round. 2 workouts + 1 PT session consumed 5 of 7 days. One good day, zero true recovery days between sessions.
