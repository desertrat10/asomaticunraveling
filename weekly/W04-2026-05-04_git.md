# Weekly Experiment Log — W04 · May 4–10, 2026

**Week descriptor:** K-tape enters the record. A deliberately gentler PT session plus taping produced the first autonomic-free PT day of the arc, five clean days, pain easing to mid-band, and a formal reclassification out of L2 — with load visibly redistributing into the un-taped zones.

---

## 1. TL;DR

This is the week something worked.

Monday opened badly — a new acute left SCM pinch on waking, near-peak at onset, painful left rotation, a familiar location that had been quiet for months. She did coffee shop work, groceries, three work blocks. Two things worth noting: a community session reduced pain during and after, logged as a novel intervention category, and she deliberately did *not* do vibration work, applying the contraindication she'd drafted the day before.

Tuesday was PT, and it was gentler by design given the flare state — suboccipitals and SCM focus, autonomic work with eye tracking and a laser, no needling, and K-tape applied to the neck and shoulders. First K-tape in the HealingOS record. And for the first time in the arc, a PT day passed with no autonomic constellation at all.

It held. Wednesday, 36 hours post-session, autonomic was still down to one brief episode on sudden standing — and that was on a day that ran heavier than planned, with heavy AI work and a 90-minute virtual meeting. Thursday was clean through disrupted sleep, four hours of floor desk, two screen sprints and another 90-minute call. Friday clean. Saturday clean, pain at its lowest in the arc, and SCM finally dropped bilaterally with the acute left pinch fully resolved.

The trade shows up in the body scan. Everywhere the tape is, the numbers fall. Everywhere it isn't — mid trap, lower trap, thoracic erectors, lumbar — they rise, and she reports tightness "below the taping line." By Friday that was showing up as overnight lower back cramps that disrupted sleep.

Day 5 the tape started to fade: soreness peaking through, which matches the 3–5 day wear window she'd set as the tracking target. She removed it Saturday afternoon, and Sunday — the first full day without it — autonomic was still clear. Only the upper trap ticked up slightly.

Sunday she cancelled the drive and the outing on capacity grounds and stayed home. The flare was formally reclassified to `elevated_baseline`, arc-end designated effective Saturday.

Two variables entered in the last two days that muddy the read: an adjunct supplement started Saturday, and cupping appeared Sunday. Both are flagged in the logs as parallel variables against the K-tape data.
