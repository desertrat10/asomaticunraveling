# Weekly Experiment Log — W05

**Week descriptor:** K-tape Cycle 2 carried a six-day autonomic-clean run at moderate-to-elevated pain through animated speech, a four-hour social block, asymmetric load and disrupted sleep — and then a thirty-minute drive produced autonomic symptoms at the twenty-minute mark with the tape still on.

---

## 1. TL;DR

The week answered two questions and opened a third.

Monday tested how long the first K-tape cycle held after removal. About eighteen hours: SCM and scalenes reactivated on the morning dog walk, and a mid-afternoon trigger flare came up in the mid-right thoracic. Pain was elevated, against moderate on the last day the tape was on. She also put in fifteen minutes of trap-targeted weights — the first direct strength work on that cluster since the tape went on.

Tuesday was PT: soft tissue, no needling, and a fresh tape application. Her own words in the log: *less pain post-PT than the past month — K-tape helping.* First time she'd attributed pain reduction to the tape directly.

Then six days with no autonomic symptoms at all, at moderate pain. Wednesday was a recovery day and the scan broadly improved, including both of Tuesday's elevated readings. Thursday brought a 75-minute animated meeting — a named trigger — and it did produce a felt physical shift afterward, but she caught it early with a microdose intervention and a second four hours later, and nothing surfaced. The log is careful about this: that clean day was intervention-supported, not tape alone.

Friday she wrote from the floor meditation chair instead of the floor desk and carried heavy groceries. She flagged the ergonomic deviation at midday as the suspect; the end-of-day scan confirmed it, with SCM and scalenes both hitting elevated — cervical-specific, nothing thoracic or lumbar. Pain back to elevated.

Saturday she dropped the cleaning and the work and kept only the four-hour friend coffee, moving and changing position throughout. The cervical cluster *improved* across a four-hour social block. She also logged something new and precise about the overnight pain: stiff under the tape, cramping below it.

Sunday broke the streak. A thirty-minute drive on a familiar suburban route, no stress, clean morning baseline — queasiness started about twenty minutes in, then vision changes, an unsettled stomach that needed ginger ale through the meeting, and cognitive hiccups where she couldn't hold conversational threads. A pain management microdose before the drive home reduced the intensity but didn't prevent it recurring. The tape was on the whole time and did not buffer it.

Cumulative therapy fatigue — roughly eight weeks of it — was named as a baseline factor three days running, independent of what the week actually demanded.

### 4b. What worked

- **K-tape Cycle 2, on the pain axis.** Her read on Tue: less pain post-PT than the past month, attributed to the tape. Three-day plateau at moderate followed.
- **Movement variation on Sat.** Four hours of animated conversation with a mentor, and the cervical cluster came *down* — SCM elevated→m+, scalenes elevated→m+, upper trap m+→m, levator scap m+→m.
- **Preemptive dosing.** Four deployments this week, three of which preceded a predicted blowback rather than following one.
- **Catching the Thu felt shift early.** The response registered before symptoms surfaced, and the intervention went in immediately.
- **The yoga pillow stretch on Mon** — reset a mid-afternoon trigger flare outright.
- **The recovery day on Wed** — five tissues improved, one worsened, six held, including both of Tuesday's elevated readings.

### 4c. What didn't work

- **K-tape against the drive trigger (Sun).** Active wear during the event. Autonomic surfaced anyway, twenty minutes in.
- **Pain management microdose pre-return-drive (Sun).** Kicked in halfway, reduced intensity, did not prevent recurrence.
- **Microdose intervention on Mon** — taken about an hour before the mid-afternoon flare, which "presented *through* the modulation."

### 4d. What made it worse

- **The 30-minute drive (Sun).** Familiar suburban route, no stress, clean baseline. Onset at ~20 minutes. The log identifies the trigger profile as cognitive load (route attention) plus motor load (steering, blind-spot and mirror checks producing cervical rotation) plus sustained cervical positioning — and names driving as a **distinct trigger class** from animated speech or extended screen.
- **The ergonomic deviation (Fri).** Writing from the floor meditation chair rather than the standard floor desk. Flagged as a suspicion at midday; confirmed by the end-of-day scan, with escalation confined to the cervical tissues.
- **Asymmetric grocery load (Fri).** Heavy basket and bag — first since Cycle 2 began — recruiting the right-side compensation chain PT is actively unpacking.
- **The 75-minute animated meeting (Thu).** Produced a felt physical response even though nothing measurable surfaced.
- **Work-week resumption after tape removal (Mon).** SCM and scalene reactivation at roughly eighteen hours post-removal.
