# Weekly Experiment Log — W09 · June 8–14, 2026

**Week descriptor:** Two proprioceptive laser sessions produced neurological spirals five days apart — one inside the post-PT window and one outside it — and the second gave her a mechanism: proprioceptive loading after tissue release, not the post-PT window itself, may be the actual contraindication.

## 1. TL;DR

The finding of this week is about the laser exercises, and it arrived in two pieces.

Tuesday was PT with fresh tape. A few hours after the session she did two minutes of proprioceptive laser work at home. Wednesday was the worst day of the week — pain 6, intense cervical fog from morning that never cleared, neurological symptoms in waves all day — on a **pure couch day with no screen work, no calls, nothing.** Zero load, worst symptoms. She named the laser exercises retrospectively: layering proprioceptive work into the post-PT recalibration window added load while the system was already recalibrating.

Sunday she did it again outside the window — three minutes of laser work after foam rollers, on a light day — and got a fast neurological spiral that put her horizontal until a pain microdose stopped the escalation. And that second occurrence changed the shape of the hypothesis. Her read, in the log: the common thread isn't the post-PT window, it's **tissue release followed by proprioceptive loading.** Foam rolling may put compensatory muscle spindle data temporarily offline the same way manual PT work does. If that holds, the contraindication is broader than anyone has been treating it — it applies after any release work, not just after a session. Two occurrences, flagged to bring to PT.

The other thing worth flagging: Wednesday brought **left-side mid-thoracic pain at the same vertebral level as the right-side tingling that was X-rayed in February** — the first left-side presentation ever at that level. It surfaced in the morning, cleared around the first pain dose, and hasn't come back. It also arrived within twenty-four hours of PT working directly on that area. And it finally puts a location on the "previously X-rayed region" she flagged back on 5/26 without specifying the level.

Around those: Monday was low, pain 4, with an outing as a passenger that produced lightheadedness and nausea afterward. Thursday settled to uniform 3s and closed the recalibration window trending down. Friday the cycle started and the floor desk block was cut short — pain too intense, moved to the couch — then a long walk, errands where the neck flared, a nap, and a few hours at the pool. She took the tape off and added a live rosin indica vape, noted as stronger and faster than flower. Saturday held at 5 through three hours of deep cleaning and three hours at a sports bar.

Autonomic symptoms on all seven days — the first fully autonomic week in the set.
