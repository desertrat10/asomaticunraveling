# Weekly Experiment Log — W09 · June 8–14, 2026

**Week descriptor:** Two proprioceptive laser sessions produced neurological spirals five days apart — one inside the post-PT window and one outside it — and the second gave her a mechanism: proprioceptive loading after tissue release, not the post-PT window itself, may be the actual contraindication.

---

## 1. TL;DR

The finding of this week is about the laser exercises, and it arrived in two pieces.

Tuesday was PT with fresh tape. A few hours after the session she did two minutes of proprioceptive laser work at home. Wednesday was the worst day of the week — elevated pain, intense cervical fog from morning that never cleared, neurological symptoms in waves all day — on a **pure couch day with no screen work, no calls, nothing.** Zero load, worst symptoms. She named the laser exercises retrospectively: layering proprioceptive work into the post-PT recalibration window added load while the system was already recalibrating.

Sunday she did it again outside the window — three minutes of laser work after foam rollers, on a light day — and got a fast neurological spiral that put her horizontal until a pain management intervention stopped the escalation. And that second occurrence changed the shape of the hypothesis. Her read, in the log: the common thread isn't the post-PT window, it's **tissue release followed by proprioceptive loading.** Foam rolling may put compensatory muscle spindle data temporarily offline the same way manual PT work does. If that holds, the contraindication is broader than anyone has been treating it — it applies after any release work, not just after a session. Two occurrences, flagged to bring to PT.

The other thing worth flagging: Wednesday brought **left-side mid-thoracic pain at the same vertebral level as the right-side tingling that was X-rayed in February** — the first left-side presentation ever at that level. It surfaced in the morning, cleared around the first pain management dose, and hasn't come back. It also arrived within twenty-four hours of PT working directly on that area. And it finally puts a location on the "previously X-rayed region" she flagged back in W07 without specifying the level.

Around those: Monday was low, moderate pain, with an outing as a passenger that produced lightheadedness and nausea afterward. Thursday settled to uniform mid-range and closed the recalibration window trending down. Friday the cycle started and the floor desk block was cut short — pain too intense, moved to the couch — then a long walk, errands where the neck flared, a nap, and a few hours at the pool. She took the tape off and added a stronger-acting wellness support variant, noted as faster than the prior form. Saturday held at moderate through three hours of deep cleaning and three hours at a sports bar.

Autonomic symptoms on all seven days — the first fully autonomic week in the set.
