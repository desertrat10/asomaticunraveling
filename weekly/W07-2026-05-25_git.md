# Weekly Experiment Log — W07 · May 25–31, 2026

**Week descriptor:** The coordinating-provider role opened at launch finally closed — new PCP established Tuesday with pain management care initiated — and a PT session described as not heavy by intensity produced the highest body scan in the record, six groups at peak, alongside right scapular spasms that quieted after the tape came off.

---

## 1. TL;DR

Two things happened this week that matter more than the pain numbers.

**Tuesday the PCP got established.** First intake. That role has been sitting `OPEN — Priority` on the roster since the beginning; it's the quarterback appointment the buildout, baseline and integration docs all point at. Pain management care was initiated at the same visit. The log flags it as a milestone, and it is — this is the thing the structured data was built to hand off to.

**Thursday's PT session produced the highest body scan in the entire record.** Levator scap, mid trap, rhomboids, lower trap, thoracic erectors and lumbar/QL all at peak, with five more groups at elevated. Pain at the top of range. The log is explicit that the session "was not heavy by intensity but included soft tissue work" — the response was disproportionate to the input.

Around those: Monday was heavy screen with complex prompting, which produced a few waves of *intense* lightheadedness. Against the prior Sunday's four-hour talking marathon that produced fog and word-finding trouble, that's two consecutive cognitive-load days with two different primary autonomic outputs — speaking load skewing fog, screen and prompting load skewing lightheadedness. Also on Monday: SCM and scalenes cleanly reversed elevated → mid with no talking load, which supports the mapping from animated speech to those specific muscles.

Tuesday she named it: "at the boundaries of exhaustion," on a roughly nine-week PT arc. She also flagged a previously X-rayed region tingling and reactivating, without specifying the level.

Wednesday was seven hours at the floor desk with a walk at noon and another at three, twenty minutes of yin, and three or four more hours of evening work. Lumbar/QL hit elevated. She cleared Thursday and Friday in advance to protect the recalibration window — which is the first time in the record the window was protected *before* the session rather than after.

Saturday the right scapular spasms were the story. Sharp, random, stabbing — muscle, not nerve — recurring in the same spot that cramped on a power walk before Thursday's PT, and continuing since. Sharp enough to stop activity until they pass. She took the tape off, partly because the skin was itchy and partly to test the correlation. The cramping noticeably quieted afterward.

Sunday closed at pain in the lower band, the week's low, with the right scapular zone still holding at elevated.
