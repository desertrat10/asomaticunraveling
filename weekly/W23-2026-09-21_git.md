# Weekly Experiment Log — W23 · 7-day window, Mon–Sun

**Week descriptor.** A five-day week anchored to a session it does not contain. The PT session on Mon (day 0) is the week's organizing event and there is no board, no wearable data, and no log for it or for Tue (+24h) — so Wed (+48h) sits inside the PT 48-hour recalibration window and there is no clean within-week baseline to measure against. The instrument also changed: this week is whiteboard and wrist-wearable screenshots, not the daily protocol, which means no body scan, no day-level HR, no sleep, no mood, and no frontmatter to cross-check. What the week does carry is three sessions with before/after/+2 boards, two of them with full HR panels, and two consecutive five-hour unprogrammed load days at the end. The single cleanest finding is a perceived-exertion inversion: the session rated *Easy* carried 29 more bpm average, broke the Zone 1 ceiling, drove pain two full points to an 8, and was the only session where the neuro score failed to return to baseline by +2 hours. The session rated *Moderate* never left Zone 1. Pain floor drifted up from 5 to 6 across the week; the measured ceiling of 8 occurred on day one, at minute 11 of a 24-minute walk.

**Number policy for this publication version.** All logged values are reported as recorded — pain scores, neuro scores, heart rate, wearable figures, session metrics, dose times and counts. **No substitute scale is used anywhere in this file.** What is withheld is the identifying layer only: names, places, calendar dates finer than the window, provider identities, medication identities and dose sizes (the two dose lines appear as A and B), and one illegible board item. Timing is given as day-of-week plus offset from the session day.
