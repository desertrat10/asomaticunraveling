# Weekly Experiment Log — W25 · 7-day window, Mon–Sun

**Week descriptor.** The first window captured on a single consistent template — one form, seven days, no mid-week format change — which makes the body scan and autonomic scan chartable across six days instead of three. It is also the window where the the prior window carry-out warning came true. Tuesday carries the worst autonomic burden in the record: eight symptoms at *interrupting*, neuro locked at 3 for four consecutive checkpoints, wake pain already at the week's ceiling, three tissue sites at *dominant*, and a new right eye pain. That day sits at +48h from the second N-PT-1 exposure, +24h from a PT session, day 3 of a reintroduced NSAID, day 4 of a second reintroduced substance — and it is the day her period started, logged as out of cycle and out of sequence. Five generators, one day, no way to rank them. From Wednesday the whole cluster resolves monotonically, and Sunday is the lowest-pain day and quietest body scan in three windows of logging. Then the lightest session in the record — a nineteen-minute floor-only Yin practice — re-triggered the vestibular pair from absent to intermittent. Generative input dropped roughly seventy percent week over week: one PT session, one short yoga session, no walk, no cardio, no N-PT-1.

**Number policy for this publication version.** All logged values are reported as recorded — pain scores, neuro scores, body and autonomic scan values, heart rate, wearable figures, session metrics, dose times and counts, and the cycle-day markers. **No substitute scale is used anywhere in this file.** What is withheld is the identifying layer only: names, places, calendar dates finer than the window, provider identities, medication identities and dose sizes (the three dose lines appear as A, B and C), the stated reason for the Wednesday load termination, and two unexpanded log terms. Timing is given as day-of-week plus offset from the session day.
