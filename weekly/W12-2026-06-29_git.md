# Weekly Experiment Log — W12 · June 29 – July 5, 2026

**Week descriptor:** Post-round maintenance holding at a low baseline across four training days, broken by a single upper-band reading on cycle day 1 (Saturday) that followed a right-turned poolside stack and the first laser proprioceptive round since the most recent round closed.

---

## 1. TL;DR

A functional week. Baseline sat at the low band most days, she trained four times, and she was in the pool and around people twice. This is the highest-capacity week in the generated set.

Monday and Tuesday held at the low band. Tuesday's 30-minute workout ended with an autonomic spiral — lightheadedness, nausea, vision changes — caught with a pain management dose before it escalated. Wednesday is missing from the record. Thursday ran heavy screen at the floor desk with an office hours call; autonomic showed up before the call, surged after it, got caught with a dose, then came back in the evening with a stiff neck.

Friday stacked: reading in the med chair, deep-cleaning a bathroom, one minute of laser proprioceptive PT exercises, then a few hours poolside in a lounge chair turned to the right. Autonomic came on after the exercises. Saturday was the spike — woke to bilateral scapular pain waves, pain at the upper band, cycle day 1. Her own logged read: cycle inflammation plus the previous day's stack, and specifically the laser PT round, which she flagged retrospectively as the first occurrence of that sequence since the neurohangover pattern right after the most recent round ended.

Sunday came back down to low-moderate with no autonomic at all — and it was a training day. The difference logged: she pre-loaded with a pain management dose before the yoga session. One occurrence.

No PT, no provider contact. The most recent round closed about twelve days before the window; the gap runs about two and a half weeks by Sunday.
