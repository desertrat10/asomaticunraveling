# Weekly Experiment Log — W11 · June 22–28, 2026

**Week descriptor:** The densest claims week in the record — a same-day A/B on blue-light lenses, a dietary attribution overturned, a confirmed pre-load stack that carried a 105-minute workshop launch to the week's lowest pain, and a bar that produced a full spiral in thirty minutes.

---

## 1. TL;DR

This week generated more usable findings than any other in the set, and most of them came from her running deliberate tests rather than from symptoms happening to her.

**The blue-light test is the cleanest single-variable result in the whole record.** Thursday morning she read texts on her phone wearing blue-light readers and got queasy. Thursday afternoon she did a clean screen session wearing plain readers and got nothing. Same person, same day, one variable. Her mechanism: chromatic distortion adding load to a visual system that's already carrying compensatory duty for degraded proprioceptive input. Action taken immediately — plain readers only for all screen work going forward.

**The lunchtime nausea got reattributed.** It had been chalked up to food sensitivity. The data this week points somewhere else entirely: morning screen-based visual load driving midday nausea, with afternoon secondary intervention calming the autonomic output before the pattern could be seen. Wednesday narrowed it further — nausea came on from reading text messages on her phone, before any computer work at all. So it isn't posture and it isn't food. It's screen and visual load itself.

**The pre-load stack worked.** Wednesday was a 105-minute workshop launch, carrying debt from Tuesday's four hours of stacked meetings on minimal sleep. Pain that day was **the week's low**, on the highest-output day of the week. Track 3 that day is the strongest entry in the record: strong energetic vibes during the workshop, regenerative and intuitive energy, deep coherence.

She also names what makes that reading dangerous: **attentional gating.** Workshop engagement quieted the neurological symptoms for the duration. Recovery cost tracking starts now. This is the same thing she named as engagement override the week before — the professional-context version of the athletic override the whole system exists to counter.

The debt landed Thursday, and it landed lower down the chain — mid trap and rhomboids both jumped from low to upper, thoracic erectors to upper with sharp right-side pains overnight. Complete exhaustion on a low-demand day, three consecutive disrupted nights.

Friday was an unplug day and produced **the first fully clean autonomic day of the logging period.**

Sunday the bar — no, Monday. The week opened with a bar visit that produced a full spiral in thirty minutes: high-mounted TV forcing sustained cervical extension, harsh window glare on the screen, non-controlled seating. Three-system mismatch. Forced departure.

And Saturday confirmed a repeat trigger: over-ear noise-cancelling headphones worn with reading glasses produce bilateral temporal compression. Temporalis at upper band.
