# Weekly Experiment Log — W11 · June 22–June 28, 2026

**Week descriptor:** This week saw a fluctuating pain range and several key observations in the autonomic and brachial systems. The primary mechanism of pain was influenced by environmental triggers and daily activities. The meditation chair was flagged as a potential contraindication, and the bar environment was confirmed as a mismatch trigger. The week ended with the patient in a recovering state, with pain levels trending downward.

---

## 1. TL;DR

- **Pain:** The pain level fluctuated between 4 and 6, with a notable drop to 4 on Wednesday (6/25) and Friday (6/27). The highest pain levels were observed early in the week, particularly on Saturday (6/28) when it reached 4–5.
- **Autonomic:** Autonomic symptoms were prominent on 5 out of 7 days, with positional lightheadedness and heavy autonomic activity noted. The pre-load stack protocol (Breathing yoga → micro pain med → golden milk → K-tape) was confirmed as effective, particularly during high-output events.
- **Brachial:** No brachial plexus involvement was reported.
- **Load Events:** The bar environment (high-mounted TV + window glare) and the meditation chair were identified as significant triggers. Over-ear headphones + reading glasses were confirmed as a bilateral temporal compression trigger.
- **Recovery:** By the end of the week, the patient showed signs of recovery, with pain levels dropping and fewer autonomic symptoms.
