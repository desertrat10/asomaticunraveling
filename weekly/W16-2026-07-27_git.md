Then Sunday cleared broadly. Pain 4, almost everything on the scan down to 2, and a two-hour coffee outing with a twenty-minute drive on each end that ran without the cognitive or conversational trouble that has shown up on every outing like it since April. The week closes with the system closer to the floor than it has been going into any session this round.
