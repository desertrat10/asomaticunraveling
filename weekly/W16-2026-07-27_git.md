# Weekly Experiment Log — W16 · July 27 – August 2, 2026

**Week descriptor:** A 40-day PT gap closed on Monday, and the six days after it were the re-loading dose — a cascade that peaked twice, once at 48 hours as fatigue and fog, once at 96 hours as a lower-back spiral that arrived in real time instead of on the delay curve.

---

## 1. TL;DR

Five weeks without hands-on care ended Monday with a first session at a new practice. The rest of the week was the body answering that session. Tuesday came in waves — pain arrived, pain management caught it, it broke through mid-afternoon. Wednesday was the low point of the front half: flu-like, foggy, a partial-only response to the pain management dose, and the floor desk unusable for the second day running. Thursday was better enough to try the gym, and the gym pushed back — the elliptical stopped on lightheadedness, the strength block after it got cut short, and the stairs on the walk home cramped the mid-back.

Friday is the day that stands out. It opened low with no hot spots, then the lower back seized in the afternoon after a seated outing and an errand, and it seized *right then* — not six to forty-eight hours later, which is how this body has worked for the entire record. Pain peaked, lumbar hit top of band, and the pain management did nothing for the tension part at all. Saturday didn't clear it, and Saturday was also the first day this week where both the upper and lower chains were loaded at the same time.

Then Sunday cleared broadly. Pain at the week's floor, almost everything on the scan down broadly, and a two-hour social outing with a twenty-minute drive on each end that ran without the cognitive or conversational trouble that has shown up on every outing like it since April. The week closes with the system closer to the floor than it has been going into any session this round.
