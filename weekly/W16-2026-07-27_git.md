# Weekly Experiment Log — W16 · July 27 – August 2, 2026

**Week descriptor:** This week marked the initial transition into the new round of endurance-focused physical therapy. The primary focus was on managing the inflammatory response and recovery arc following a new provider's hands-on session. The patient experienced elevated pain levels and autonomic symptoms, particularly around the 48-hour and 72-hour post-PT marks. By the end of the week, the system showed signs of recovery, with pain and tissue activation levels returning closer to baseline.

## 1. TL;DR

The week began with the first hands-on PT session with a new provider, which led to a prolonged inflammatory response and elevated pain levels. The peak of this response was observed 48 hours post-PT, with autonomic symptoms and positional lightheadedness. By the end of the week, the patient had started to recover, with pain levels and tissue activation decreasing. Notable interventions included the use of meloxicam, K-tape, and micro pain doses. Air quality index (AQI) exposure was a recurring factor, particularly on days when the patient experienced exhaustion despite adequate sleep.
