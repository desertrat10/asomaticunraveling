# Weekly Experiment Log — W24 · 7-day window, Mon–Sun

**Week descriptor.** The first complete 7-of-7 window in the record, and the first with tissue and autonomic scans in it. Pain ran lower than the prior window on every measure — the floor returned to 5 and held there five days of seven, the ceiling dropped from 8 to 7, and the mean of day averages fell about a quarter point. That improvement sits alongside the largest neuro event in two weeks of logging, and the two are not in tension: the event came from an exercise with no mechanical load at all. One round of N-PT-1 gaze fixation, right and left at 3-second intervals, drove the neuro score from 1 to 3 inside ninety minutes and produced a tail that outlasted every session in the record. The cross-modality exertion inversion replicated to the bpm. Two PT days produced the same pain curve twice. The week closes with maximum confounding: two medications reintroduced in the final 48 hours, a second N-PT-1 exposure stacked on a yoga session, and an open neuro tail.

**Number policy for this publication version.** All logged values are reported as recorded — pain scores, neuro scores, tissue and autonomic scan values, heart rate, wearable figures, session metrics, dose times and counts. **No substitute scale is used anywhere in this file.** What is withheld is the identifying layer only: names, places, calendar dates finer than the window, provider identities, medication identities and dose sizes (the three dose lines appear as A, B and C), and one unexpanded board abbreviation. Timing is given as day-of-week plus offset from the session day.
