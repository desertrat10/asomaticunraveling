**Week descriptor:** Seven consecutive days inside a single post-intervention window (dry needling + bounded e-stim, right-side cervical target), run at baseline with no flare — six self-directed sessions, a stair-stepper threshold that landed one beat off the reading taken on the same machine twenty-two days earlier, a pain distribution that migrated caudally and then crossed the midline for the first time, and self-rated effort running **monotonically inverse to measured heart rate across every rated session in the week.**

**The week perceived exertion stopped tracking anything.**

**Fri · +96h** — No session. An asymmetric two-bag carry from vehicle to residence **drove the day's heart rate above every programmed session peak of the week and well above the measured cardio threshold.** The premenstrual cluster at peak. Primary care established; a specialist referral pending.

**Mon · day 7** — Clinical PT session and driving. New template in first use: the ordinal autonomic scan picks up **positional lightheadedness and spatial disorientation on postural transitions**, both intermittent. Heaviest body scan of the week — five groups at *dominant*.

**Window totals:** pain held a two-point band with no day at its ceiling-plus, and no flare classification triggered. One day logged good, six middling, zero bad. Subjective state ran good-to-neutral all seven days and was explicitly decoupled from pain on at least three.

1. **The entire window sits inside one post-intervention window** — days one through seven — **and the index session's own daily log was not supplied to this build.** Every interval claim in this file is anchored to a session whose data is not in hand.
2. **Cycle phase and post-intervention window are perfectly confounded.** Late luteal across all seven days, premenstrual cluster at peak from +96h, onset late. There is no day in this window where one runs without the other. Note also that the index day was already inside the luteal phase, so phase alone does not explain the resting-rate jump.
3. **A second espresso roughly two hours before the +48h session** — the hottest session, the only +2 exit.
4. **The +72h three-variable divergence** — the one-hour speaking block, dose-curve position (yoga near peak, cardio in the tail), and modality itself. Not separable from a single day.
5. **Resting heart rate captured on three of seven days.** The cycle-vs-window hypothesis has a four-day hole in the middle of its own test window.
6. **Day 5's wearable data undercounts** — an unwatched morning walk. Steps, distance, stand hours, idle hours and activity calories are all low. Not comparable to the rest of the window.
7. **Day 7's body scan timing** relative to the clinical session is unrecorded, so the window's heaviest scan cannot be assigned to pre- or post-session state.
8. **The template change on day 7** means the intervention ledger, the autonomic track and the Track 3 series all break at the last day of the window.

## 9. Data Integrity Notes

1. **The supplied window is Tue–Mon, not Mon–Sun.** On the standing grid, this is W21 **minus its Monday** plus the **first day of W22**. The file is built on the days supplied and labeled W21 for continuity. Two consequences: the index session day — which anchors every interval claim here — falls outside the file, and the final day will double-count if W22 is later built conventionally. Flagged rather than resolved.

2. **Frontmatter remains unusable for query.** The autonomic flag reads false on six of seven days while the bodies record a premenstrual cluster at peak, exhaustion at peak, an elevated cardiovascular response, lightheadedness and spatial disorientation. The yoga-session flag reads false on four of the five yoga days. The threshold field is blank on the one day a threshold was captured in the body. Unchanged from the prior synthesis.

3. **Two files carry wrong or duplicated frontmatter.** One daily opens with a complete frontmatter block belonging to a different day, followed by its own correct block. Another carries the following day's date in frontmatter while every heading and all content read as its own day. Two files append a blank copy of the daily template below the completed entry, including a duplicate header.

4. **Body scan unscored on three of seven days**, including the window's highest-symptom day. The scan matrix is 4/7 complete.

5. **Internal conflict in the +96h file.** The highlights state the carry-event heart rate exceeded the window's highest programmed session peak by a margin that does not match the two values recorded elsewhere in the same file; the Track 3 note in that same file identifies the correct comparison peak. The corrected difference is used throughout this synthesis.

6. **Arithmetic conflict in the day-6 file.** The log characterizes the yoga peak as having declined for a fourth consecutive session. The series supports three consecutive declines after the +48h session. Three used here; the observation itself holds.

7. **Zone boundary inconsistent within a single day.** The lowest heart-rate zone is recorded with one upper bound for the first session and a different one for the second, ninety minutes later — and differently again across the week. Likely a wearable re-estimate, but it means the zone is not a fixed band across the window's session data.

8. **Block-level capture incomplete on day 5.** Logged blocks account for roughly a third of the session's total duration; one block has no duration at all. Block codes are unassigned on every yoga session this window, as in the prior synthesis — so the twists question stays unanswerable.

9. **Wearable data absent entirely on the two-session day.** Session data captured, no daily totals, no resting rate. On day 7 it appears as an addendum marked as having no template field. Resting heart rate captured on only three of seven days.

10. **No modality checklist on days 1–6.** Ice, heat, percussion, S-hook, foam roller, TENS and KTape have no field until the day-7 template. §4a's physical-modality rows are template artifacts, not an absence of use, and are not comparable to the prior synthesis.

11. **Driving untagged at +96h** despite a round trip of roughly twenty-five minutes each way recorded in the highlights. The travel flag reads false. The carry event was tagged with a hand-added non-template field.

12. **Day 7 logs no session detail** despite the clinical-session flag reading true — no session block, no notes, no entry/exit numbers, no highlights content. The window's only clinical session has no record beyond the tag, the scan, and the intervention list.

13. **Evening pain missing on day 6; mid-morning pain missing at +96h.** Day-level neuro missing on three days; session entry/exit missing on day 6.

14. **Protocol version changed mid-window without a transition note.** Day 7 introduces the ordinal autonomic scan, the intervention checklist with dose times, and numeric body-scan entry; it drops the highlights block, Track 3, the session blocks, and the split-autonomic detail section. The autonomic series is not continuous across days 6 and 7 and should not be charted as one line.

15. **Contraindication registry roughly five months stale.** No entries for the HR-gated cardio or yoga protocols. The provoking posture now has two logged provocations and a working substitution and should be entered.
