# Neuro Storm Case Report

*60-second gaze fixation exercise → 72+ hour neurological cascade → flare extending beyond six days*

---

## Incident Summary

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Date** | ~October 3, 2026 |
| **Trigger activity** | Gaze fixation exercise from a concussion/TBI vestibular rehabilitation protocol |
| **Duration of trigger** | 60 seconds total (multiple positions × 3-second holds) |
| **Exercise description** | Fixate on thumb held at varying eccentric gaze positions; minimal load, minimal eccentricity |
| **Symptom onset** | Immediate |
| **Acute storm duration** | 72+ hours |
| **Current status** | Day 6 flare as of October 9, 2026 |
| **Systemic impacts** | Autonomic cascade + endocrine disruption (menstrual cycle triggered weeks early) |

---

## Protocol Origin

The exercise came from a physical therapist sourcing it from a concussion recovery program designed for traumatic brain injury. The stated goal: endurance training for visual-vestibular integration.

The operating assumption of that protocol family: the brain needs retraining — a software bug. The channels are presumed structurally intact; the central processor just needs practice integrating them.

This presentation does not have a TBI. It has six degenerated cervical discs producing erroneous proprioceptive afferents. Hardware, not software.

---

## Symptom Cascade

### Hours 0–24

- Immediate lightheadedness and spatial disorientation
- Blurred / soft vision, persistent
- Nausea emerging
- Cognitive fog — inability to sustain attention
- Autonomic activation — cardiovascular and temperature dysregulation

### Hours 24–72

- Symptoms did not plateau; oscillated without resolution
- Sleep severely disrupted
- Nausea persistent
- Positional exacerbation — any head movement amplified the cascade
- Endocrine disruption: menstrual cycle triggered weeks early

### Days 4–6

- Acute storm subsided to elevated baseline; flare continuing
- Post-activity lightheadedness pattern persists
- Spatial disorientation on postural transitions
- Fatigue — recovery window extending well beyond the typical 24–48 hour recalibration

## Mapping to Li et al. (2022) Circuitry

Step by step through Li's Figure 1 pathway:

1. **Sustained visual fixation at eccentric gaze positions** (5–20°) with the head/neck held static — loading the cervico-ocular reflex (COR) pathway under conditions where ocular fixation is the dominant task.

2. **Li notes symptoms are "reproduced by a specific neck movement or position rather than a whole-body movement"** (refs 4, 70). Eccentric static holds with sustained ocular fixation sit squarely inside that provocation zone.

3. **The cervical channel emitted corrupted afference** from four moderate-plus degenerated levels; the visual channel asserted a fixed target; the mismatch resolved at the **vestibular nuclei**.

4. **From there:** vestibular nuclei → reticular formation and parabrachial nucleus → sympathetic preganglionic neurons → cardiovascular and GI outflow (refs 39, 40). Nausea, spatial disorientation, autonomic cascade — Li's vestibulo-sympathetic reflex pathway, documented two years before this unintentional experiment triggered it.

5. **Right temporalis/SCM amplification maps to a specific Li finding:** spontaneous EMG activity of myofascial trigger points is "consistent with hyperactive muscle spindles" (ref 50). A chronically hypertonic right SCM is plausibly not just guarding — it may be a proprioceptive noise generator in its own right, recruited further during the mismatch event.

## Dosage Finding

Li describes episodes lasting minutes to hours (ref 8). This ran 24+.

That is not a contradiction — it is amplitude. A three-second hold at minimal eccentricity producing a 24-hour-plus cascade is n=1 evidence that the afferent noise floor sits well above what the review's typical patient population carries. It converts "the exercise was too much" into "the exercise exposed how far outside the reference population the threshold sits."

Context note: this occurred mid PT Round 2, during the compensation-release window documented in the HealingOS baseline — 25 years of right-side bracing being dismantled, new stability still under construction. The provocation threshold is presumably lowest precisely in this window. That interaction — release-induced destabilization lowering the storm threshold — is itself undocumented territory.

---

## Systemic Spillover: Endocrine Disruption

The menstrual cycle was triggered weeks early. This is the most novel data point in the case.

Li documents autonomic symptoms (palpitations, nausea, vomiting) via the vestibulo-sympathetic reflex pathway (refs 6, 7). The HPG axis sits downstream of autonomic regulation. But nowhere in Li — nor in months of cervicogenic dizziness literature review — is endocrine disruption documented as part of the cascade.

Two possible readings:

1. **Higher amplitude, wider recruitment.** The afferent noise floor in this presentation is high enough to recruit downstream pathways beyond what typical cervicogenic dizziness engages.
2. **Reporting gap.** Endocrine spillover occurs but is not measured or reported in the existing literature, which focuses on vestibular/autonomic outcomes.

Either reading supports the thesis: the compensation-release destabilization window has pushed this presentation into a higher-amplitude regime than the literature describes.

## Implications for the SCED Design

1. **Provocation pathway validated.** The exercise class Li identifies as symptom-provoking did provoke symptoms — dramatically. The diagnostic model works for this presentation.

2. **Threshold quantification.** If 60 seconds produces 72+ hours, baseline JPE and posturography measurements must start at lower intensity and shorter duration than standard protocols suggest. The safe-dose window is narrower than the literature implies.

3. **Endpoint expansion.** If the cascade recruits endocrine systems, HRV alone is an insufficient autonomic marker. Cycle timing, sleep architecture, and fatigue severity become relevant secondary measures.

4. **Protocol screening rule.** No concussion-derived protocols. Every proposed exercise is vetted through one question: does this load the COR under static conditions with sustained fixation? If yes, it is out. Breath-linked dynamic movement only.

## What This Incident Confirms / Does Not Confirm

**Confirms:**

- Concussion protocols are structurally mismatched to degenerative multi-level DDD presentations
- The vestibulo-sympathetic reflex pathway is active and recruitable in this case
- The provocation threshold sits far below what WAD-derived expectations suggest
- Recovery windows can extend far beyond the minutes-to-hours duration Li describes

**Does not confirm:**

- Whether endocrine spillover is unique to this case or underreported generally
- Whether the multi-day cascade duration is specific to the compensation-release window or characteristic of the presentation baseline

Both remain open for documentation.
